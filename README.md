## Design System and Brand

- [NSW Design System](https://designsystem.nsw.gov.au/) - Core styles, components, patterns and guidance for NSW Government websites and services.
- [NSW Design System Source](https://github.com/NSWGTP/nsw-design-system) - Sass, JavaScript and starter kit behind the design system, published to npm as `nsw-design-system`.
- [Figma UI Kit](https://designsystem.nsw.gov.au/get-started/figma-ui-kit.html) - How to get and use the official Figma library of design system components.
- [NSW Government Brand Framework](https://www.nsw.gov.au/branding/nsw-government-brand-framework) - Branding principles and the five brand categories, from masterbrand to stand-alone.
- [NSW Email Toolkit](https://email.digital.nsw.gov.au/) - Templates, components and guidance for accessible, on-brand government email.
- [Public Sans](https://public-sans.digital.nsw.gov.au/) - Downloads and usage guidance for the masterbrand typeface.
- [@nswds/tokens](https://github.com/digitalnsw/nswds-tokens) - Colour, spacing and typography tokens for CSS, Sass, JavaScript, Tailwind, Figma and DTCG.
- [@nswds/ui](https://github.com/digitalnsw/nswds-ui) - React component library for NSW Government digital products.

## Standards and Strategy

- [Digital Service Toolkit](https://digital.nsw.gov.au/delivery/digital-service-toolkit) - Standards, templates and guides for designing and delivering government digital services.
- [NSW Design Standards](https://digital.nsw.gov.au/delivery/digital-service-toolkit/design-standards) - Ten standards for designing and delivering NSW Government digital services.
- [Delivery Manual](https://digital.nsw.gov.au/delivery/digital-service-toolkit/delivery-manual) - Phase-by-phase guide to agile service delivery, from pre-discovery to retirement.
- [Activities and Templates](https://digital.nsw.gov.au/delivery/digital-service-toolkit/activities-and-templates) - Research and design methods with ready-to-use templates.
- [Customer Commitments](https://www.nsw.gov.au/nsw-government/state-of-customer/commitments) - Six service commitments the NSW Government makes to the people it serves.
- [NSW Digital Strategy](https://digital.nsw.gov.au/strategy) - Whole-of-government digital missions, roadmap and case studies.
- [Government Technology Platforms](https://digital.nsw.gov.au/delivery/government-technology-platforms) - Shared platforms agencies can reuse, such as the Customer Payments Platform and Licence.NSW.
- [NSW AI Assessment Framework](https://digital.nsw.gov.au/policy/artificial-intelligence/ai-governance-assurance-and-frameworks/nsw-ai-assessment-framework) - Mandatory framework for assessing the risks of government AI systems.
- [AI Guidance and Tools](https://digital.nsw.gov.au/policy/artificial-intelligence/ai-guidance-and-tools) - Practical guidance for agencies adopting artificial intelligence.
- [Cloud Policy](https://digital.nsw.gov.au/policy/cloud-policy) - Whole-of-government policy and circulars for adopting cloud services.
- [Digital Assurance](https://digital.nsw.gov.au/policy/digital-assurance) - Framework for assuring government digital and ICT projects.

## Content

- [Content Style Guide](https://digital.nsw.gov.au/delivery/digital-service-toolkit/resources-and-guides/writing-content/content-style-guide) - Grammar, punctuation, approved terms, numbers, dates and interface text conventions.
- [Content Design](https://digital.nsw.gov.au/delivery/digital-service-toolkit/resources-and-guides/writing-content/content-design) - Introduction to the content design practice for government services.
- [Support for Agencies on nsw.gov.au](https://www.nsw.gov.au/nsw-government/onecx-program/support-for-agencies-on-nswgovau) - Content principles, lifecycle and CMS component guide for publishing on the central government website.
- [Easy Read](https://digital.nsw.gov.au/delivery/accessibility-and-inclusivity-toolkit/communication/easy-read) - When and how to produce Easy Read versions of information.
- [Australian Government Style Manual](https://www.stylemanual.gov.au/) - Standard for Australian Government writing and editing.
- [Inclusive Language](https://www.stylemanual.gov.au/accessible-and-inclusive-content/inclusive-language) - Writing respectfully about people, from the Style Manual.

## Accessibility

- [Accessibility and Inclusivity Toolkit](https://digital.nsw.gov.au/delivery/accessibility-and-inclusivity-toolkit) - Guidance on designing, building, buying and testing accessible government services.
- [Fundamentals of Digital Accessibility](https://digitalnsw.github.io/fundamentals-of-digital-accessibility/) - Self-paced introductory course.
- [Accessibility Testing Basics](https://digitalnsw.github.io/accessibility-testing-basics/) - Self-paced course on manual testing techniques.
- [Creating Accessible Documents](https://digitalnsw.github.io/creating-accessible-documents/) - Self-paced course on making documents everyone can use.
- [Accessibility in Digital Procurement](https://digitalnsw.github.io/accessibility-in-digital-procurement/) - Self-paced course on building accessibility requirements into what you buy.
- [How to Meet WCAG](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/quickref/) - Filterable quick reference to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines 2.2.
- [Guidelines on Equal Access to Digital Goods and Services](https://humanrights.gov.au/resource-hub/by-resource-type/guidelines-and-standards/guides-and-standards-disability-rights/guidelines-equal-access-digital-goods-and-services) - Australian Human Rights Commission guidance on disability discrimination obligations online.

- [Spatial Collaboration Portal](https://portal.spatial.nsw.gov.au/portal/apps/sites/#/homepage) - Spatial datasets and web mapping services from NSW Spatial Services.

- [Open Data Policy](https://www.data.nsw.gov.au/data-policy/nsw-government-open-data-policy) - Open-by-default principles for releasing government data.
- [Information Classification, Labelling and Handling Guidelines](https://data.nsw.gov.au/data-policy/nsw-government-information-classification-labelling-and-handling-guidelines) - Rules for classifying, labelling and handling sensitive government information.

## Buying and Funding

- [buy.nsw](https://buy.nsw.gov.au/) - Marketplace where government buyers and suppliers connect.
- [ICT Purchasing Framework](https://www.info.buy.nsw.gov.au/resources/ICT-Purchasing-Framework) - Mandatory contract framework for buying ICT goods and services.
- [ICT Services Scheme](https://www.info.buy.nsw.gov.au/schemes/ict-services-scheme) - Prequalification scheme for ICT and digital service suppliers.
- [Test and Buy Innovation](https://digital.nsw.gov.au/delivery/test-and-buy-innovation) - Agile procurement guidance and pathways for buying innovative solutions.
- [Digital Restart Fund](https://digital.nsw.gov.au/funding/digital-restart-fund) - Funding program for government digital and ICT initiatives.
- [NSW Gateway Assurance](https://www.nsw.gov.au/nsw-government/public-sector/financial-information-for-public-entities/nsw-gateway-assurance) - Independent reviews at key decision points in a government project's lifecycle.

## Security, Privacy and Identity

- [Cyber Security NSW](https://digital.nsw.gov.au/delivery/cyber-security-nsw) - Lead agency for government cyber security strategy, incident response and guidance.
- [Cyber Security Policy](https://digital.nsw.gov.au/delivery/cyber-security-nsw/policies) - Cyber security requirements, circulars and directives for agencies.
- [Essential Eight](https://www.cyber.gov.au/essential-eight) - Australian Signals Directorate baseline mitigation strategies and maturity model.
- [Information Security Manual](https://www.cyber.gov.au/ism) - Australian Signals Directorate framework of cyber security controls.
- [Privacy for Agencies](https://www.ipc.nsw.gov.au/privacy/agencies) - Information and Privacy Commission guidance on agency obligations under NSW privacy law.
- [Guide to Privacy Impact Assessments](https://www.ipc.nsw.gov.au/resources/guide-guide-privacy-impact-assessments-nsw) - How to run a privacy impact assessment on a new project or system.
- [Mandatory Notification of Data Breach Scheme](https://www.ipc.nsw.gov.au/privacy/MNDB-scheme) - Agency obligations for assessing and reporting eligible data breaches.
- [NSW Digital ID](https://www.nsw.gov.au/nsw-government/digital-identity-and-cybersecurity/nsw-digital-id) - Government digital credential for verifying identity online.
- [Sector Link](https://www.nsw.gov.au/departments-and-agencies/premiers-department/sector-link/for-agencies) - Cross-agency identity federation and single sign-on for government staff and applications.
